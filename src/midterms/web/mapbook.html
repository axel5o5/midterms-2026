How Holdout was built

The first version pulled red-state Democrats back toward their state's lean and carried over part of each state's 2024 polling miss, both estimated on all the data at once. Before trusting either, each candidate adjustment was tested on elections it hadn't seen.

Holdout, my model. Senate: the Silver Bulletin poll average averaged with Split Ticket's margin, then moved by 54% of the state's 2024 presidential polling miss beyond the national miss when polls underrated Democrats, or 26% when they underrated Republicans. The old 42% weight for both directions stays as another version. Senate margins are simulated with national (SD 3.0), state (2.7) and race errors. That carry-over was the only adjustment that predicted past polling misses when tested one midterm year at a time (2002-2022); a pull toward the state's lean and a longer state record did not, so they are shown only as other versions. House: Split Ticket's margin, averaged with district polls where there are two or more. No governor model.
